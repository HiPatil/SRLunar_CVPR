\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Latent prediction.} I-JEPA~\cite{assran2023self} predicts the representations of masked image blocks from a visible block, with targets from an exponential moving average (EMA) of the encoder, a teacher also used by BYOL, DINO, iBOT and data2vec~\cite{grill2020bootstrap,caron2021emerging,zhou2022ibot,oquab2024dinov2,baevski2022data2vec}. V-JEPA and V-JEPA~2 do the same for video~\cite{bardes2024revisiting,assran2025vjepa}, and V-JEPA~2.1~\cite{murlabadia2026vjepa} adds supervision at four depths and a distance-weighted loss on visible tokens, both of which we adopt. Multimodal masked modeling~\cite{bachmann2022multimae,mizrahi20234m} predicts across modalities but reconstructs pixels or tokens. Predicting one modality's latents from another's dates back at least to audio-visual RAVEn~\cite{haliassos2023jointly}. In Earth observation the closest mechanism is Galileo's~\cite{tseng2025galileo}. Its predictor queries carry the target channel group and cross-attend to the other groups, with EMA targets, but inputs are resampled to 10\,m and the loss is contrastive. AnySat~\cite{astruc2025anysat} predicts fused multi-sensor cell embeddings. X-JEPA, CR-JEPA and Le MuMo JEPA~\cite{choudhury2026xjepa,hossain2026crjepa,cornelissen2026mumo} predict or regularize pooled embeddings, mostly for retrieval. To our knowledge no JEPA conditions its predictor on the illumination geometry of the target. The nearest precedents condition on the month (Galileo), the target view's projection~\cite{perera2025crossjepa} or a caption~\cite{huang2026text}.

\paragraph{Collapse and its diagnosis.} Joint-embedding methods can collapse completely or into a low-dimensional subspace~\cite{jing2022understanding,hua2021feature,tian2021understanding}. VICReg~\cite{bardes2022vicreg} and Barlow Twins~\cite{zbontar2021barlow} decorrelate features across a batch. C-JEPA~\cite{mo2024connecting} adds VICReg to I-JEPA. LeJEPA~\cite{balestriero2025lejepa} replaces the teacher with a sliced test toward an isotropic Gaussian (SIGReg), whose gradients can vanish under collapse~\cite{wu2026visreg}. RankMe~\cite{garrido2023rankme} and LiDAR~\cite{thilak2024lidar} estimate quality from the embedding spectrum, and Latent MIM~\cite{wei2024towards} monitors the cosine between pooled embeddings of different images. Objectives can also be met without content. Context prediction learned patch location from chromatic aberration~\cite{doersch2015unsupervised}, and JEPAs trained with VICReg can latch onto slow, trivially predictable features~\cite{sobal2022joint}. CAPI~\cite{darcet2025cluster} reports positional collapse, in which latent masked-image models predict the position of masked tokens instead of their content. It was found by inspecting feature maps and fixed by clustering targets per position. StoP~\cite{bar2024stochastic} and SiamJEPA~\cite{yamada2026siamjepa} make positions stochastic or shuffle the teacher. OlmoEarth~\cite{herzog2026olmoearth} replaced EMA targets, which collapsed in its experiments, with a frozen random projection. Absolute position also leaks through zero padding~\cite{islam2020much,kayhan2020translation,chu2023conditional}, and self-supervised ViTs, including ones with rotary embeddings, carry input-independent positional components~\cite{pawlowsky2026dino,yang2024denoising}. We analyze positional collapse in a continuous-target, cross-modal JEPA, derive which monitors and regularizers should miss or reward it, and propose a monitor that can see it.

\paragraph{Resolution in Earth observation models.} Most multi-sensor models resample their inputs to a common grid~\cite{fuller2023croma,xiong2024neural,jakubik2025terramind,tseng2025galileo}. The alternatives are GSD-scaled positional codes~\cite{reed2023scale,irvin2023usat,forgaard2026thor} and per-band or per-sensor patch sizes~\cite{irvin2023usat,wang2025towards}. OmniSat and AnySat~\cite{astruc2024omnisat,astruc2025anysat} cut all modalities into the same ground cells, and we follow them. They fuse a cell's modalities into one token, however, which rules out predicting one product from another. Location encoders on the sphere~\cite{mai2020multi,klemmer2025satclip,vivancocepeda2023geoclip,russwurm2024geographic} provide a geographic prior. We use Slepian functions~\cite{simons2006spatiospectral}, as proposed for location encoding by Rao~\etal~\cite{rao2026localized}.

\paragraph{The Moon and other planets.} Lunar machine learning has mostly meant task-specific models. Examples are crater detection~\cite{silburt2019lunar,yang2020lunar,tewari2022automated}, rockfall and boulder mapping~\cite{bickel2019automated,bickel2020impacts,aussel2025global}, imaging of permanently shadowed regions~\cite{bickel2021peering} and single-view topography~\cite{chen2022cnn,sander2026moon}. The NASA-IBM lunar foundation model~\cite{fraccaro2026multimodal} retrains TerraMind's masked-token recipe on WAC and NAC imagery and topography from SomBench~\cite{patil2026sombench}. It takes acquisition geometry as encoder input, and static maps enter it as tile-averaged scalars. LunarFM~\cite{gironamata2026lunarfm} is a MultiMAE over 18 global channels on 15\,km chips and names JEPA-style objectives as future work. Moonstone~\cite{prasad2026moonstone} is a modality-grouped MAE at 237\,m. On Mars, MOMO~\cite{purohit2026momo} merges per-sensor MAEs, and Mars-Bench~\cite{purohit2025marsbench} standardizes evaluation. None of these models predicts latents across modalities. \method keeps static maps as spatial inputs and targets at native resolution, and learns from repeat observations under different suns.
