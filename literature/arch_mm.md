## Closest prior work to cross-modal latent prediction between co-registered modalities

Short answer: predicting one co-registered modality's latents from another is **not new**. It has been done in Earth observation (EO) since 2025 and in audio-visual learning since 2023. The novelty case for LeLunar has to rest on the specific design and the lunar domain, not on the objective itself.

1. **tseng2025galileo (Galileo, ICML 2025): the single closest method I found.** Its global objective builds a context view and a target view from *disjoint* channel groups (modalities) of co-registered EO data: "repeating steps (a-b) for the target encoder while excluding overlapping channel groups". It predicts EMA-target-encoder representations, and the exit depth depends on the modality: linear projection for pseudo-labels, the full encoder for S1/S2, half the encoder for the others. Its predictor cross-attends to the visible tokens and is queried with the target tokens' position, time, **month** and channel-group embeddings.
   - How it differs from LeLunar: the loss is contrastive (PatchDisc), not regression; the context contains 2 to 17 channel groups rather than a single source; there is no conditioning on illumination geometry and no MoE; the data is terrestrial. The month embedding is the nearest precedent for conditioning the predictor on the target observation's acquisition state.
2. **herzog2026olmoearth (OlmoEarth, CVPR 2026)** shares authors with Galileo. It uses modality-aware masking that includes "full modality reconstruction", so some modalities are decode-only targets. Its targets are frozen *random* linear projections ("Latent MIM Lite") trained with a modality-aware patch-discrimination loss. The authors report "training instability, representation collapse" when replicating existing work, and say the fixed target encoder avoids "the representation collapse common in Latent MIM-style training". This is the most directly relevant counter-position to LeLunar's EMA-target design and its collapse issues.
3. **choudhury2026xjepa (X-JEPA, WACV 2026), hossain2026crjepa (CR-JEPA, arXiv 2026), hossain2026hqjepa (HQ-JEPA, arXiv 2026)** are two-modality (Sentinel-1/Sentinel-2, or RGB/Sentinel) cross-modal JEPAs.
   - X-JEPA predicts "the semantic embedding of a target modality given context from another" and is aimed at retrieval.
   - CR-JEPA is architecturally close to LeLunar: modality-specific stems, a shared trunk, and masked latent prediction within and across modalities, plus SIGReg.
   - X-JEPA's claim to be "the first to extend joint embedding predictive paradigms beyond unimodal domains" is contradicted by earlier multimodal JEPAs (AnySat, CVPR 2025; M3-JEPA, ICML 2025) and by JEPA-style cross-modal latent prediction (RAVEn, ICLR 2023). Do not repeat it.
4. **astruc2025anysat (AnySat, CVPR 2025)** is a multimodal EO JEPA. Its student predicts EMA-teacher embeddings of *dropped* patches after a modality-combiner fuses all modalities, plus a contrastive modality-consistency loss. Its targets are fused multimodal patch embeddings, not a named target modality's grid.
5. **Audio-visual (temporally co-registered):**
   - haliassos2023jointly (RAVEn, ICLR 2023): the audio student predicts both audio and video momentum-teacher targets, and the video student predicts audio targets. This is the earliest EMA-teacher cross-modal latent prediction I found.
   - lian2023avdata2vec and huang2023mavil: contextualized cross-modal targets.
   - teotia2026mjepa (MJEPA, ECCV 2026): shows that cross-modal prediction is "critical" for a shared encoder, but its cross-modal targets are *pooled*.
6. **Token or pixel space on co-registered modalities (not latent targets):** bachmann2022multimae, mizrahi20234m, bachmann20244m, jakubik2025terramind, hou2023mask3d, nedungadi2024mmearth, fuller2023croma. The idea itself goes back to zhang2017split (cross-channel prediction) and tian2020contrastive (CMC).
7. **Other cross-modal JEPAs:**
   - lei2025m3jepa: an MoE *predictor*.
   - perera2025crossjepa: frozen teacher; predictor conditioned on the target view's projection geometry.
   - nguyen2026cross4djepa: dense cross-modal distillation from a frozen teacher.
   - chen2026vljepa, robson2026avjepa.
   - yang2026thermojepa (Thermo-JEPA, ECCV 2026): aligned RGB is used as privileged structure for a thermal JEPA teacher.
   - huang2026text (TC-JEPA, ICML 2026) conditions the predicted patch features on captions "to reduce the prediction uncertainty". That is the same rationale as conditioning LeLunar's predictor on target sun geometry.

**What I could not find, and so the defensible novelty claims (phrase them as "to our knowledge"):**
- (a) Conditioning a JEPA predictor on the *target observation's illumination or acquisition geometry* (via AdaLN-Zero plus an extra KV token). The nearest precedents are Galileo's month embedding, CrossJEPA's projection conditioning and TC-JEPA's text conditioning.
- (b) Dense, full-token-grid prediction of a single named target modality from a single masked source modality at a 4096-token scale, across many planetary raster modalities.
- (c) Sparse MoE in the *encoder* of a cross-modal JEPA.
- (d) Deep supervision combined with visible-token dense self-distillation in that setting.
- Lunar multimodal foundation models are covered in the sibling `lunar.bib` (e.g. gironamata2026lunarfm, prasad2026moonstone, sander2026moon, fraccaro2026multimodal). `jepa_ssl.bib` also lists cornelissen2026mumo (MuMo JEPA, CVPRW 2026) and murlabadia2026vjepa (V-JEPA 2.1, dense video features; its ECCV 2026 chapter exists per CrossRef). **I did not verify those entries.** Check them before claiming (b).

**Position shortcut / "position-code collapse":**
- The closest documented analogue is darcet2025cluster (CAPI). It describes "positional collapse": early models' patch embeddings were dominated by position. CAPI fixes it by making the clustering targets carry no positional information.
- Other related evidence: bar2024stochastic (StoP: I-JEPA-style models overfit to location features), yang2024denoising (positional-embedding artifacts in ViT features), wei2024towards (trivial solutions in latent MIM, "high region correlation") and yan2026mitigating (decoders over-rely on positional information).
- None of these documents *cross-sample* position-code collapse in a cross-modal JEPA specifically.

## Unverified items and caveats

- **"Data-efficient cross-modal distillation"**: I could not identify a specific paper by this description. I included the canonical cross-modal distillation papers gupta2016cross (supervision transfer RGB to depth/flow, CVPR 2016) and aytar2016soundnet (NIPS 2016). A recent candidate is sbrolli2026synesthesia (ECCV 2026), which is motivated by scarce paired data. Tell me the intended paper if it is a different one.
- **Receptive-field misalignment / half-pixel offsets in strided convs or ViT patch embeddings**: I found **no paper specifically about half-pixel misalignment of conv-stem outputs relative to the ViT patch grid**. The nearest verified sources:
  - araujo2019computing (receptive-field centre offsets under padding and stride)
  - wu2019convolution (even kernels shift the feature map; symmetric padding fixes it)
  - alsallakh2021mind (uneven padding under stride causes spatial bias)
  - huang2020devil (pixel-coordinate/flip bias from half-pixel conventions)
  - he2017mask (RoIAlign quantization misalignment)
  - odena2016deconvolution (uneven overlap, checkerboard artifacts)
  - rojasgomez2024making (tokenization breaks ViT shift-equivariance)

  I found no canonical paper on the `align_corners` convention. The clearest treatment I found is a blog post (Bart Wronski, 2021, bartwronski.com), which is not cited.

## Entries


### Multi-modal and cross-modal self-supervised learning

- **bachmann2022multimae**: Masked autoencoding over co-registered RGB, depth and semantic maps; per-modality patch projections and decoders predict all modalities from a sparse subset of visible patches across modalities. *Relation:* Canonical cross-modal masked prediction on pixel-aligned modalities. It predicts pixels, not EMA latents, and its extra modalities are pseudo-labels. *Verified:* https://www.ecva.net/papers/eccv_2022/papers_ECCV/html/7102_ECCV_2022_paper.php; https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.01678
- **mizrahi20234m**: One Transformer encoder-decoder trained by masked modeling over many modalities, each mapped to discrete tokens by a modality-specific tokenizer. *Relation:* Any-to-any cross-modal prediction in discrete-token space. LeLunar predicts continuous EMA-teacher token grids instead. *Verified:* https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/hash/b6446566965fa38e183650728ab70318-Abstract-Conference.html; https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.06647
- **bachmann20244m**: Scales 4M to tens of modalities, including DINOv2/ImageBind feature maps and metadata as tokenized targets. *Relation:* Precedent for treating other networks' feature maps as prediction targets across modalities. *Verified:* https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/71883294314045d60c900113a359934b-Abstract-Conference.html; https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.09406
- **girdhar2023imagebind**: Binds six modalities into one embedding space by contrastive alignment of each to images. *Relation:* Global contrastive binding with no dense cross-modal prediction; a contrast to LeLunar's dense grid targets. *Verified:* https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Girdhar_ImageBind_One_Embedding_Space_To_Bind_Them_All_CVPR_2023_paper.html; https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.05665
- **weinzaepfel2022croco**: Cross-view completion: reconstruct masked pixels of one view given a second view of the same scene. *Relation:* Completes a target from another observation of the same scene, in pixel space. Close in spirit for geometry-oriented pretraining. *Verified:* https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2022/hash/16e71d1a24b98a02c17b1be1f634f979-Abstract-Conference.html; https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.10716
- **weinzaepfel2023croco**: CroCo v2: larger-scale cross-view completion with RoPE, used for stereo and optical flow. *Relation:* Uses RoPE in a cross-view ViT, which is relevant to LeLunar's 2D RoPE; still pixel targets. *Verified:* https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2023/html/Weinzaepfel_CroCo_v2_Improved_Cross-view_Completion_Pre-training_for_Stereo_Matching_and_ICCV_2023_paper.html; https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.10408
- **girdhar2022omnivore**: One ViT classifies images, videos and single-view 3D (RGB-D), with modality-specific patch embedding. *Relation:* Precedent for a shared trunk with per-modality patch stems. *Verified:* https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2022/html/Girdhar_Omnivore_A_Single_Model_for_Many_Visual_Modalities_CVPR_2022_paper.html; https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.08377
- **girdhar2023omnimae**: A single masked autoencoder pretrained jointly on images and videos. *Relation:* Modality-agnostic masked pretraining with a shared encoder. *Verified:* https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Girdhar_OmniMAE_Single_Model_Masked_Pretraining_on_Images_and_Videos_CVPR_2023_paper.html; https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.08356
- **jaegle2021perceiver**: A latent array cross-attends to arbitrary input arrays, iteratively. *Relation:* Background for cross-attention as a modality-agnostic interface. *Verified:* https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/jaegle21a.html; https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.03206
- **jaegle2022perceiver**: Perceiver IO adds output queries decoded by cross-attention, giving structured outputs of arbitrary size and semantics. *Relation:* Query-based cross-attention decoding of an output grid, which is essentially the pattern of LeLunar's predictor (target-grid queries attending to context tokens). *Verified:* https://iclr.cc/virtual/2022/poster/6269; https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.14795
- **lu2023unified**: Unified-IO: one sequence-to-sequence model over images, text and dense outputs via discrete tokens. *Relation:* Cross-modal translation in token space. *Verified:* https://iclr.cc/virtual/2023/poster/12725; https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.08916
- **lu2024unified**: Unified-IO 2: autoregressive any-to-any model over vision, language, audio and action. *Relation:* Large-scale cross-modal translation baseline; generative rather than representation-oriented. *Verified:* https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Lu_Unified-IO_2_Scaling_Autoregressive_Multimodal_Models_with_Vision_Language_Audio_CVPR_2024_paper.html; https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.17172
- **baevski2022data2vec**: Predicts contextualized EMA-teacher latents (average of top-K blocks) of masked inputs; the same recipe works for speech, vision and text. *Relation:* EMA latent targets built from multiple teacher layers; each model sees one modality. *Verified:* https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/baevski22a.html; https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.03555
- **baevski2023efficient**: data2vec 2.0: does not encode masked tokens, uses a fast convolutional decoder, and amortizes teacher targets over several masks. *Relation:* Efficiency practices for latent-target prediction. *Verified:* https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/baevski23a.html; https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.07525
- **assran2023self**: I-JEPA: predicts EMA target-encoder representations of several target blocks from one context block, using a narrow ViT predictor. *Relation:* Direct ancestor. LeLunar swaps within-image target blocks for other modalities' token grids. *Verified:* https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Assran_Self-Supervised_Learning_From_Images_With_a_Joint-Embedding_Predictive_Architecture_CVPR_2023_paper.html; https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.08243
- **bardes2024revisiting**: V-JEPA: feature prediction on video with spatio-temporal multi-block masking and an L1 loss. *Relation:* JEPA at scale with a regression loss; the reference design for masking and the predictor. *Verified:* https://openreview.net/forum?id=QaCCuDfBk2; https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.08471
- **assran2025vjepa**: V-JEPA 2: JEPA pretraining on more than 1M hours of video, then an action-conditioned predictor for planning. *Relation:* Current large-scale JEPA reference (*preprint*). *Verified:* https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.09985
- **bardes2023mcjepa**: MC-JEPA: a joint-embedding predictive architecture that learns optical flow (motion) and content features together. *Relation:* A JEPA with a dense (flow) objective alongside content (*preprint*). *Verified:* https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.12698
- **lei2025m3jepa**: M3-JEPA: multimodal alignment in latent space with a multi-gate mixture-of-experts predictor, trained with contrastive and regularization losses. *Relation:* The only JEPA I found that uses MoE, and it places the MoE in the *predictor*. LeLunar places sparse MoE in the encoder. *Verified:* https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/lei25b.html; https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.05929
- **choudhury2026xjepa**: X-JEPA: cross-modal JEPA for remote-sensing retrieval; predicts the target modality's embedding from the other modality's context (BEN-14K S1/S2, fMoW RGB/Sentinel) with a Prediction Space Alignment loss. *Relation:* Close in objective: cross-modal latent prediction between co-registered RS modalities. It is two-modality and retrieval-oriented, and its 'first beyond unimodal' claim is contradicted by earlier works. *Verified:* https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/WACV2026/html/Choudhury_X-JEPA_A_Novel_Joint_Learning_Cross-Modal_Predictive_Alignment_Framework_for_WACV_2026_paper.html
- **hossain2026crjepa**: CR-JEPA: modality-specific stems, a shared transformer trunk, and JEPA objectives predicting masked latent targets within and across modalities, with SIGReg on retrieval projections. *Relation:* Architecturally the closest to LeLunar: per-modality stems, a shared trunk, and within- plus cross-modal latent prediction. Limited to two modalities (S1/S2) (*preprint*). *Verified:* https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.00706
- **hossain2026hqjepa**: HQ-JEPA: masked latent prediction on paired S1/S2, plus cross-modal token alignment, SIGReg and a quantum-fidelity similarity loss. *Relation:* Cross-modal masked latent prediction on co-registered SAR/optical (*preprint*). *Verified:* https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.31068
- **teotia2026mjepa**: MJEPA (ECCV 2026): one shared encoder for audio and video, with intra-modal JEPA against EMA targets plus cross-modal predictors on pooled features. *Relation:* Evidence that cross-modal prediction is needed for a shared encoder. Its cross-modal targets are pooled, whereas LeLunar's are dense grids. *Verified:* https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-36969-7_6 (CrossRef: Computer Vision - ECCV 2026, LNCS, pp. 97-114); https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25225
- **yang2026thermojepa**: Thermo-JEPA (ECCV 2026): JEPA pretraining for a thermal video world model that injects spatial topology from aligned RGB (privileged information) as a confidence-gated prior into the thermal teacher's attention; releases the RGBT-World corpus. *Relation:* Uses a co-registered RGB modality to shape a latent-prediction teacher for another modality, and reports preventing representation collapse. Method known from the Springer abstract only; no arXiv version found. *Verified:* https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-032-37013-6_7 (CrossRef: Computer Vision - ECCV 2026, LNCS, pp. 115-132)
- **sbrolli2026synesthesia**: Synesthesia / DLA (ECCV 2026): uses undecoded generative latents of synthesized modalities as privileged information, and distils them into a visual-only student with a predictive (not matching) objective (MESSy). *Relation:* A recent cross-modal distillation method motivated by scarce paired data. Possibly what 'data-efficient cross-modal distillation' referred to (unconfirmed). *Verified:* https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-37020-4_20 (CrossRef: Computer Vision - ECCV 2026, LNCS, pp. 362-379); https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08336
- **robson2026avjepa**: AV-JEPA: extends LeJEPA to audio-visual learning, using modality dropout as masking and SIGReg; no EMA teacher. *Relation:* An EMA-free alternative for cross-modal latent alignment (*preprint*). *Verified:* https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15295
- **perera2025crossjepa**: CrossJEPA: predicts frozen image-foundation-model embeddings of rendered views from point clouds, with the predictor conditioned on cross-domain projection information. *Relation:* A cross-modal JEPA whose predictor is conditioned on target-view geometry, analogous to conditioning on target illumination. It uses a frozen teacher, not EMA (*preprint*). *Verified:* https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.18424
- **nguyen2026cross4djepa**: Cross4D-JEPA: dense per-point distillation of frozen 2D foundation-model features (DINOv2 / V-JEPA 2) into a 4D point encoder via projection correspondences. *Relation:* Dense cross-modal latent targets. It finds that correspondence granularity drives the gain (*preprint*). *Verified:* https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.00514
- **chen2026vljepa**: VL-JEPA: a vision-language model that predicts continuous embeddings of target texts instead of generating tokens. *Relation:* Cross-modal latent prediction (vision to text embeddings) at an ICLR 2026 venue. *Verified:* https://openreview.net/forum?id=tjimrqc2BU (ICLR 2026 Poster); https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.10942
- **huang2026text**: TC-JEPA: modulates I-JEPA's predicted patch features with a sparse cross-attention text conditioner, to reduce prediction uncertainty at masked positions. *Relation:* The nearest analogue to conditioning LeLunar's predictor on target sun geometry: side information that explains target variance the context cannot. *Verified:* https://openreview.net/forum?id=bmvWC1ainA; https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.03245
- **balestriero2025lejepa**: LeJEPA: JEPA theory plus Sketched Isotropic Gaussian Regularization (SIGReg); no stop-gradient or teacher. *Relation:* The regularizer used by CR-JEPA, HQ-JEPA and AV-JEPA; an alternative to EMA-based collapse prevention (*preprint*). *Verified:* https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.08544
- **zhang2017split**: Split-brain autoencoders: two sub-networks each predict one channel subset from the other (e.g. L and ab, RGB and depth). *Relation:* The earliest cross-channel prediction between co-registered channels. Targets are pixels or quantized values; there is no latent teacher. *Verified:* https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_cvpr_2017/html/Zhang_Split-Brain_Autoencoders_Unsupervised_CVPR_2017_paper.html; https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.09842
- **tian2020contrastive**: CMC: contrastive learning where the views are different channels or modalities of the same scene (L, ab, depth, normals, flow). *Relation:* Early SSL across co-registered modality views; contrastive rather than predictive. *Verified:* https://www.ecva.net/papers/eccv_2020/papers_ECCV/html/1453_ECCV_2020_paper.php; https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.05849
- **gupta2016cross**: Supervision transfer: train a depth or flow network to match mid-level features of an RGB-trained teacher on paired images. *Relation:* Cross-modal feature regression on co-registered pairs with a fixed teacher. LeLunar uses a co-trained EMA teacher instead. *Verified:* https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_cvpr_2016/html/Gupta_Cross_Modal_Distillation_CVPR_2016_paper.html; https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.00448
- **aytar2016soundnet**: Distills visual recognition networks into a sound network using unlabeled video. *Relation:* Classic cross-modal distillation through natural pairing. *Verified:* https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2016/hash/7dcd340d84f762eba80aa538b0c527f7-Abstract.html; https://arxiv.org/abs/1610.09001
- **huang2023mavil**: MAViL: masked audio-video reconstruction, intra- and inter-modal contrastive losses, and self-training on contextualized audio-video features. *Relation:* Uses cross-modal contextualized latent targets. *Verified:* https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/hash/40b60852a4abdaa696b5a1a78da34635-Abstract-Conference.html; https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.08071
- **gong2023contrastive**: CAV-MAE: extends MAE to audio plus video and adds cross-modal contrastive learning. *Relation:* Joint masked modeling and contrastive learning across modalities. *Verified:* https://openreview.net/forum?id=QPtMRyk5rb; https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.07839
- **haliassos2023jointly**: RAVEn: masked audio and video encoders predict momentum-encoder targets. The audio stream predicts audio and video targets; the video stream predicts audio targets. *Relation:* The earliest EMA-teacher cross-modal latent prediction I found. Its asymmetric choice of targets parallels LeLunar's source/target sampling. *Verified:* https://openreview.net/forum?id=BPwIgvf5iQ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.06246
